# Food Delivery Operations & Customer Analytics

A Power BI and SQL-based business analytics project focused on understanding
revenue drivers, delivery performance, restaurant performance, and customer
retention for a food delivery business.

The project combines SQL analysis, Power Query, data modeling, DAX, and
Power BI visualization to convert operational data into actionable business
insights.

---

## Business Problem

A food delivery business needs to understand what drives revenue, where
delivery performance is creating operational risk, and whether customers
are returning frequently enough to sustain long-term revenue.

This project analyzes transactional, customer, restaurant, and delivery
data to identify key performance gaps and opportunities for improvement.

---

## Business Objectives

- Identify the restaurants and customer segments contributing the most revenue
- Measure overall order and customer performance
- Evaluate on-time delivery performance
- Understand repeat-customer behavior
- Compare restaurant and category performance
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Identify operational bottlenecks affecting customer retention
- Translate analytical findings into actionable recommendations

---

## Key Business Questions

1. Which restaurants generate the highest revenue?
2. What is the overall revenue and order volume?
3. How many customers are being served?
4. What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
5. What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
6. Which categories contribute the most revenue?
7. Which restaurants combine high revenue with slower delivery?
8. How does revenue change month over month?
9. What operational improvements could strengthen customer retention?
