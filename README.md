# Food Delivery Operations & Customer Analytics

Power BI analytics project focused on understanding revenue drivers,
delivery performance, customer retention, and operational risk in a
food delivery business.

## Business Problem

The objective was to identify the operational and customer-behavior
factors affecting revenue, delivery performance, and repeat purchases,
and translate the analysis into actionable business recommendations.

## Key Business Questions

- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
- Which restaurants and categories drive revenue?
- Where are delivery-time bottlenecks affecting customer experience?
- What relationship exists between delivery time, ratings, and revenue?
- How strong is customer repeat behavior?
- Which areas represent the biggest retention opportunity?

## Tools & Technologies

- SQL — KPI calculation, aggregation and business analysis
- Power Query — data transformation and preparation
- Power BI — data modeling, visualization and dashboard development
- DAX — calculated measures and KPIs
- Generative AI — hypothesis generation and insight synthesis

## Analytical Workflow

SQL
→ Data Preparation
→ Power Query
→ Data Modeling
→ DAX Measures
→ Power BI Dashboard
→ Insight Validation
→ Business Recommendations

## Key Insights

- Only 30% of orders were delivered on time, indicating a significant
  delivery-performance risk.
- Repeat-customer rate was 46%, highlighting an opportunity to strengthen
  customer retention.
- Several high-revenue restaurants showed slower delivery performance,
  creating a potential customer-experience risk.
- 53% of revenue came from regular customers, indicating both strong
  customer value and dependency on repeat business.

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides visibility into:

- Revenue drivers
- Order performance
- Customer segments
- Delivery performance
- Restaurant performance
- Monthly revenue trends
- Customer retention indicators

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize delivery-time improvements for high-revenue restaurants.
2. Investigate operational bottlenecks affecting on-time delivery.
3. Introduce targeted loyalty initiatives to increase repeat purchases.
4. Monitor high-value repeat customers as a key retention segment.

## AI-Assisted Analysis

Generative AI was used as an analytical copilot for hypothesis generation
and insight synthesis.

All AI-generated insights were validated against the underlying data
and dashboard metrics before being included in the final analysis.
